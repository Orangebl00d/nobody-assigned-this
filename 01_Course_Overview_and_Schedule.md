# Nobody Assigned This
### A two-week course in agency, for someone who's already good at homework

## The big idea

You're very good at doing what's asked. This course is about the other skill: noticing what's worth doing when nobody asks, then doing it anyway, even when you might be told no or look a little foolish. AI can now write the essay, build the website, and summarize the book in seconds. It can't pick which problem deserves your Saturday, hit send on the email you're nervous about, or own what happens after. That part is yours, and you're going to practice it for real.

## What you'll walk away with

- A shipped project that at least one real person outside your family has used, bought, attended, or acted on
- At least one cold email sent to someone you admire (a reply is a bonus; sending is the skill)
- A real responsibility at home that you run on your own judgment
- A working method for AI: you decide, it builds, you judge
- A before/after score on 10 specific behaviors, so you can see what changed

## How each session works

Every session has the same shape: a short **hook**, a **reading** (5 minutes, tops), **discussion** with your parent or mentor, an **exercise**, then a **challenge** to do in the real world before next time, plus a few **journal** questions. Minutes are listed for every block. If you're working solo, write answers to two of the discussion questions in your journal.

**Three rules for the whole course:**
1. A parent reviews every message to a stranger before you send it.
2. No spending money without a parent's OK.
3. AI help is always disclosed where it matters. Never hand in AI work as your own for school.
