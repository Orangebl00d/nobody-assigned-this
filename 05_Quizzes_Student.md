# Quiz Sheets

No notes, no AI, about 10 minutes each. These test judgment rather than memory, so there's often a *best* answer instead of a perfect one. Explain your thinking wherever you can.

---

## Quiz 1 (after Session 3)

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________

**1. Which best describes agency as this course uses it?**
- a) Being the smartest person in the room
- b) Doing assigned work quickly and well
- c) Deciding something is yours to act on, and finding a way without waiting for instructions
- d) Never asking anyone for help

**2. Your friend says: "I'd start a podcast about sneakers, but everyone at school would think it's cringe." Which low-agency belief is this, and what's the most useful first response?**
- a) "I don't know what to do." Send them a podcasting tutorial.
- b) "I'm afraid of what people will think." Ask them to name exactly who "everyone" is.
- c) "I'm afraid the action will go wrong." Tell them podcasts rarely fail.
- d) None. They're just being realistic about their audience.

**3. Your group project partner vanished two days before it's due. They haven't answered texts since Monday.**
Write a **Level 2** response and a **Level 5** response. For the Level 5, say who you'd tell and why.

Level 2: ____________________________________________

Level 5: ____________________________________________

**4. What level is this message to a coach?**
> "Our warmups run 25 minutes and we only get the gym for 90. I timed it three practices in a row. I think it's because we wait for everyone to arrive before starting. We could start with whoever's there, shorten warmups to 15, or move stretching to before we enter the gym. I'd start with whoever's there, because it costs nothing. Can we try it Thursday?"
- a) Level 2
- b) Level 3
- c) Level 4
- d) Level 5

**5. Which situation most clearly calls for asking permission first?**
- a) Emailing a scientist you admire with one question about their research
- b) Correcting a typo on your club's website when you already have edit access
- c) Posting a group photo from a friend's party to your public account
- d) Signing up for a free workshop at the library

**6. What's the biggest problem with this cold email?**
> "Hi! I'm 15 and super passionate about design. Could you mentor me? I'd love to pick your brain. Thanks!!"
- a) It's too short.
- b) It mentions the sender's age.
- c) It could have been sent to anyone, and the ask is huge and vague.
- d) It uses exclamation points.

**7. DHH says "Agency is rarely given. It's taken, then recognized." Which is the best example of that done well?**
- a) Taking over your group's project because you know you'd do it better
- b) Starting a study group nobody organized, running it well for three weeks, and having the teacher start recommending it
- c) Waiting to be named team captain before helping organize practices
- d) Ignoring your coach's plays because you think yours are better

**8. Short answer (4–6 sentences).** Pick one real problem from your Problem Log and write a complete Level 4 message about it to the person who could say yes. Include the problem with one fact, the cause, two or three options, your pick and why, what you need, and by when.

## Quiz 2 (before Demo Day)

**Name:** ______________ **Date:** ______________

**1. Dickie Bush says high-agency people "decide what to do themselves, then use AI to help them achieve it." Which is the best example?**
- a) Asking AI "What should my capstone be?" and doing the first idea it suggests
- b) Choosing to build a practice tracker for your team after three teammates complained, writing what it must do, then using AI to write the code
- c) Doing everything by hand so AI doesn't make you lazy
- d) Having AI write your Level 4 message and sending it without reading it

**2. In Paul Graham's "dead code" scenario, the problem is:**
- a) AI writes bad code.
- b) Professors shouldn't use AI to grade.
- c) Humans who only pass AI output along add nothing, so they could be removed without changing the result.
- d) Compilers are getting too powerful.

**3. You used AI to help build your website. Which statement passes Shipper's "I did this" test?**
- a) "The AI made the site. I just told it to."
- b) "I decided it was for parents scanning a flyer, cut the fake testimonials the AI added, rewrote the pricing to match what I'll actually charge, and chose to launch with one page instead of four."
- c) "I made the site myself with no help."
- d) "The AI and I made it together, 50/50."

**4. A friend asks you to "just use AI" to write their history essay, since "that's what high agency means now: using the tools." What's the best response?**
- a) Agree. Using tools is high agency.
- b) Refuse and report them.
- c) Point out that handing in AI work as your own breaks the rules and skips the thinking the essay is for. Offer to show them how you use AI to quiz yourself or get feedback on your own draft instead.
- d) Write it yourself for them instead.

**5. You emailed a local architect about your capstone. Seven days, no reply. What's the best move?**
- a) Email every day until they answer.
- b) Assume it's a no and never contact them again.
- c) Send one short, polite follow-up restating your single question, then move on if there's still no answer.
- d) Find their personal phone number and call.

**6. The owner of a bakery reads your pitch and says, "This isn't really what we need. Our problem is catering orders." What kind of no is this, and what's your next move?**
- a) Not now. Check back in a month.
- b) Not this. Ask what the catering problem looks like and whether you could help with that instead.
- c) Not me. Ask who else to talk to.
- d) It's a yes. Start building the original idea.

**7. Which is "ship it ugly" done right?**
- a) Sending a teacher an apology email full of typos because speed matters
- b) Launching your one-page study planner to five classmates with just the core feature working, and asking them what's missing
- c) Posting a half-finished video about a classmate before checking the facts
- d) Skipping testing on the bike you repaired because you can fix it if it breaks

**8. Which is a sign that "agency" has turned into something worse?**
- a) You fixed the family calendar without being asked and told everyone.
- b) You keep reorganizing the group project your way even though two teammates have asked you to stop.
- c) You sent three cold emails in one day.
- d) You asked your parent for a "use your judgment" zone.

**9. Your science group's presentation flopped because the slides you made didn't load. Rewrite this into an ownership statement using the four-part repair:**
> "The school Wi-Fi is garbage. It's not my fault it didn't load."
