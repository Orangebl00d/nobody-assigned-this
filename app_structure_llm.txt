## Notes
- Source of truth is the Markdown files 00-07. nobody-assigned-this.html is generated by build/build_page.py
  (needs the `markdown` pip package) using build/page_template.html.
- Nobody_Assigned_This_FULL_COURSE.md is a plain concatenation of 00-07; regenerate with cat when files change.
- build/build_page.py writes index.html + nobody-assigned-this.html (full docs, for Pages) and
  build/artifact.html (fragment, no doctype/head). Republish build/artifact.html to the artifact URL above;
  the artifact adds its own head skeleton.
