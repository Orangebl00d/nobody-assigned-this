## Per-Session Prompts

**Session 1: Every Noun**
- What were you telling yourself in the 10 seconds before you started the Unasked Fix?
- Which point of the high-agency triangle did you use? Which was missing?
- How much did you actually need help, versus how much you thought you would?

**Session 2: Whose Problem Is This?**
- How did the person react to your Level 4? What does that tell you?
- What was hardest: finding the cause, picking an option, or sending it?
- Where were you tempted to stop at Level 2?

**Session 3: Nobody's Holding the Permission Slip**
- What happened to the fear when you wrote down actual names?
- What was your most ridiculous-in-hindsight worry before you hit send?
- Did you make a forgiveness/permission call you'd make differently now?

**Session 4: Pilot, Not Passenger**
- What did the AI get wrong that you caught? What might you have missed?
- Which decision in the sprint was the hardest?
- Finish it: "I did this because I decided ___."

**Session 5: Ship It Ugly**
- What did your tester do that you didn't expect?
- When did you want to explain or defend instead of listening?
- What could you cut even further?

**Session 6: It's Mine**
- What was the first decision you made in your zone? Did you want to ask permission anyway?
- Which promise did you almost break? What made the difference?
- What did v0.2 fix? What did it reveal?

**Session 7: Build Check**
- What was your blocker, and which rung of the ladder got you past it?
- What part are you proudest of? What part would you hide if you could?

**Session 8: Demo Day**
- Which behavior score moved the most, and why?
- What would Day-1 you think of what you shipped?
- What's the next thing nobody's going to assign you?
